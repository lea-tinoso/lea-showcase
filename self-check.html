Agentic Ladder Self-Check

Grade yourself on the four levels of agentic coding. Tick only what you can back with real work, write down the evidence, then copy your result and send it back.

The ladder is the "Agentic Coding Proficiency Journey" from a 15-minute talk by two Senior Staff Engineers at Airbnb ↗. Lea graded herself with it first. See her result.

You're looking at Lea's example answers. Press "Start fresh" to grade yourself.

How to grade honestly

  1. Count only what you touched.Your own commits and decisions. Keep them apart from the commits your agents made under your name.
  2. Don't count other people's work.Not your teammates', and not your agents', even inside tools you use every day.
  3. Let an AI take the first pass, then correct it.It will over-credit you. Lea corrected hers twice, and both corrections lowered the score.
  4. A level counts when every box is ticked.Some boxes on the next level means one foot in it, not the level.
